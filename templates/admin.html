Create Demo Account

Make a separate demo account for one person — a free 7-day full trial. Only you can give Lifetime access. Tell the person their login afterwards.

Leave the password blank and a random 16-character password is generated and shown once — share it with the person right away, it is never shown again.

Signed-up Users ({{ users|length }})

Press Access to set how long someone's access lasts — Demo (7 days), 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, Lifetime, or a custom number of months; the timer starts the moment you pick. Press Block to end someone's access at once — their data and plan stay untouched, and Unblock restores them.
